Cathedrals of the Collective: Cooperative Wineries as Civic Infrastructure in Southern France (1900–1970)

An open atlas of the cooperative circuit

More than 550 cooperative wineries were built in Languedoc Roussillon in the twentieth century. This prototype maps them, follows the circuit that linked growers to urban cooperatives, and sets out the hypotheses of a doctoral research.
